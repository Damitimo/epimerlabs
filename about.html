Built for industries where mistakes actually matter

Epimer Labs exists because the organizations that need AI most are the ones that can least afford to get it wrong.

Our Story

Why we exist

Epimer Labs was founded on a simple observation: the industries with the most to gain from AI are the ones where it is hardest to ship. Banks, pharma companies, hospitals, and insurers do not get to move fast and break things. They have regulators, auditors, and customers whose trust took decades to earn.

Most AI initiatives in these organizations stall in the gaps. Strategy decks that never become products. Proofs of concept that cannot survive a compliance review. Models bolted onto workflows nobody wants to use.

We close those gaps by working as one team. Strategy, design, and engineering under one roof, carrying a product from first insight to production code without handoffs that lose the plot along the way.

Working with Toyota, RBC, TD, GSK, and Servier taught us how to ship inside organizations where every decision is scrutinized. That discipline is our product.

Our Mission

We help enterprises in regulated industries turn AI ambition into working products: designed for trust, engineered for scrutiny, and shipped to production.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Humans at the center

AI products succeed when real people trust and adopt them. Every decision prioritizes the end user while supporting the workflows and goals of the organization around them.

Regulatory fluency

We treat compliance as a design input, not an obstacle. Understanding regulatory requirements and operational realities is part of the craft, not someone else's problem.

Evidence over hype

Our work is grounded in research, data, and measurable outcomes. We evaluate models before we trust them and validate products with users before we scale them.

Accessible by default

Products that only work for some people are unfinished. We build to accessibility standards as a baseline and design for the full range of people who will actually use the product.

Transparency & partnership

We work as an extension of our clients' teams, sharing knowledge generously and building capabilities within their organizations.

Our Approach

How we think about building AI products

Design for the high-stakes moment

In regulated industries, users often meet your product at moments of consequence: a loan decision, a treatment question, a claim. Our designs provide clarity exactly when it matters most.

Clarity over cleverness

Consequential information must be immediately understandable. We choose plain language and clear hierarchy over design trends that sacrifice usability.

Honest about uncertainty

AI systems are probabilistic. Interfaces that hide that fact erode trust the first time the model is wrong. We design products that communicate confidence honestly.

Respect privacy and dignity

The data behind AI products is often deeply personal. Every design and engineering decision considers the sensitivity of that information and the people it belongs to.

Augment expertise, never replace judgment

The best AI products make experts faster and more confident. We build systems that support professional judgment, not systems that pretend to substitute for it.

Production is the finish line

A demo is not a product. We consider the entire lifecycle: evaluation, guardrails, monitoring, and the operational reality of running AI systems in production.
