FAQ

Your questions, answered

We specialize in regulated industries, where shipping AI is as much about trust, auditability, and compliance as it is about capability. Strategy, design, and engineering work as one team, so the product that gets scoped is the product that gets built.

We work with enterprises and scale-ups in regulated and high-stakes industries: banking and financial services, pharma and life sciences, healthcare, insurance, mobility, and enterprise software. We are comfortable navigating complex corporate structures while keeping startup pace.

Compliance is built into our process from day one. Whether the constraint is HIPAA, GDPR, financial services regulation, or accessibility standards like WCAG, we design and engineer with those requirements in mind throughout every phase, not as an afterthought. This reduces risk and avoids costly rework late in the project.

We start with a discovery call to understand your needs and recommend the best engagement model. Once we define the project scope, we assemble the ideal team, create a detailed plan, and kick off with clear milestones and timelines.

We deliver working products, not proofs of concept that stall in a slide deck. Every engagement is scoped around outcomes your organization actually measures: adoption, efficiency, risk reduction, and revenue. We define those success metrics with you at kickoff and report against them throughout.
